A Web-Based Demand-Driven Medicine Expiry Management System Using FEFO-Based Dispensing

Medicine expiry in the Kenyan healthcare sector remains a persistent challenge, with facility-level waste rates reaching up to 32%. This project implements a proactive, closed-loop prototype tailored for Kenyan Level-4 Public Hospitals. By tracking medicine inventory at the batch level, the framework automates the evaluation of shelf-life parameters to eliminate manual indexing errors.
