What the system does

FEFO dispensing

Automatically recommends the earliest-expiring batch. Any override requires a logged justification.

Expiry alerts

Daily scans flag batches approaching expiry at 90, 60, and 30 days.

Demand forecasting

A 3-month moving average of dispensing history, calculated per medicine.

Procurement recommendations

Triggered when projected stock falls below a 30-day safety threshold, with a two-step approval flow.

Role-based access

Pharmacists, store managers, procurement officers, and admins each see what they need.

Audit trail

Every dispensing action, override, and configuration change is recorded.
