First Expiry First Out (FEFO) Inventory Principle

The system prioritises eligible medicine batches with the earliest expiry dates during dispensing. Expired, depleted and inactive batches are excluded from normal FEFO selection. This supports appropriate stock rotation and reduces medicine expiry-related wastage.
