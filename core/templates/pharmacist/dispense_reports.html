Operational Interpretation & Recommendations

Practical implications of dispensing and consumption records.

01. FEFO Discipline

Review override transactions and their documented reasons. Consistent FEFO application helps reduce the likelihood of older eligible stock remaining unused until expiry.

02. Medicine Consumption

Frequently dispensed medicines provide useful consumption evidence. These records can support demand forecasting and replenishment decisions.

03. Dispensing Records

Ensure every dispensing operation is accurately recorded, including the medicine, quantity, selected batch and dispensing operator.

04. Expiry Prevention

Dispensing activity should be considered alongside batch expiry dates, remaining stock and near-expiry alerts to identify medicines requiring timely stock management.
