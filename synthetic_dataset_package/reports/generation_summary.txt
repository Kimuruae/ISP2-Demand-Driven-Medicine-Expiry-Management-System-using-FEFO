IMPORTANT METHODOLOGICAL NOTES
------------------------------------------------------------
November forecasts were calculated before the 1–9 November snapshot transactions.
Demand forecasting uses a three-period moving average of completed monthly demand.
November FEFO-eligible stock must remain valid through the maximum 14-day prescription period.
Procurement target stock uses daily forecast demand multiplied by supplier lead time plus the 30-day review period plus safety stock days.
Recommended procurement quantity is the positive difference between target stock and projected stock.
November snapshot transactions were excluded from the November forecast history.
Expired batches may retain positive quantities but are not eligible for FEFO dispensing.
The confidence score measures consistency, not forecast probability.
